## Hero prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE ultra-wide 16:9 cinematic automotive still for the hero of an exquisitely art-directed independent premium car care website. A sculptural graphite silver performance grand touring sedan, physically plausible contemporary German-influenced design but NO identifiable brand, NO logos, NO license lettering, parked in a vast perfectly dark photographic cyclorama. Low camera at front wheel level, front three-quarter view from driver side, car nose points towards lower left, car occupies right 70% of frame and lower half with the rear extending to the right edge. Left upper 40% is seamless pure near-black negative space for website headline. Long razor-sharp softbox white specular reflections trace the hood, roof and muscular wheel arch; meticulously machined large alloy front wheel, thin white headlight. Automotive advertising photography by a meticulous luxury photographer, medium format, optical realism, satin metallic paint, refined contrast, dark charcoal floor with faint grounded reflection. Deep near-black background #090b0c. Silver tones only, crisp controlled light, no coloured neon, no red lights, no smoke, no fog, no racetrack, no city, no showroom, no garage, no people. The image should feel like a quiet museum sculpture, with extremely high craft. No text anywhere. The car must read clearly through silver highlights, not disappear into darkness. Wide landscape composition.
